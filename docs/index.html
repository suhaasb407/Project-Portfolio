About

I'm Suhaas. I like engineering at the scale of hands: parts you can hold, mechanisms you can feel, and sensors small enough to fit inside a surgical tool.

I'm a mechanical engineering student at Purdue University (GPA 3.80, Dean's List). My work sits where mechanical design meets electronics and code. I design the housing, lay out the PCB that drives it, and write the Python that makes it move.

Right now I'm an undergraduate researcher in Purdue's Mechanisms and Robotic Systems (MARS) Lab, designing compact hardware for vision-based tactile sensors used in robot-assisted minimally invasive surgery. I'm also a subteam lead in ASME Biomechanics, designing the ramp for the SAE Civic Action wheelchair project, after building an active upper-limb orthosis with the club last spring. Before that I led a team of 10 engineers on EPICS MOBI, building a rugged, rechargeable beeping ball for visually impaired baseball players.
